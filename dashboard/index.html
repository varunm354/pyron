How to read NASA evidence here

NASA observations

Recorded BASS-II measurements and excerpts from NASA documents. These describe experiments in microgravity, not the Pyron prototype.

Project interpretation

Pyron's damper response is a concept informed by NASA findings on airflow and flames. Closing dampers is not shown to guarantee flame extinction.

Prototype calibration

MQ-2 thresholds and servo angles need separate physical calibration. NASA data does not supply MQ-2 ADC thresholds.

  • This imported table contains no explicit flame-spread measurements. The NASA documents searched above do report flame-spread results.
  • Microgravity results do not directly validate performance in lunar or Martian partial gravity.
  • The cardboard habitat demonstrates sensing and actuation; it does not reproduce microgravity combustion.
  • NASA CO and CO₂ measurements are not used to calibrate the MQ-2 sensor, and live readings are not overlaid on NASA data.
  • Airflow values in the configuration are provisional prototype estimates, not NASA measurements.
  • Damper angles are servo commands, not independently measured positions.
  • Gas readings are raw MQ-2 ADC counts, not calibrated concentrations.
